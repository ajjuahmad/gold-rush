Gold Rush

Competitive coin arena for 2 to 4 players. Add bots if friends are away.

How to play. Pick up coins (gold ones are worth 3) and carry them back to the glowing BASE in your colour to bank them. The more you carry, the slower you move. Press Space (or the Dash button) to lunge: if you hit a rival outside their base you steal half of what they carry and the rest spills. Most banked coins after 90 seconds wins.
